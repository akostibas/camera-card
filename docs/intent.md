# Product intent

This is how we judge whether a change moves the product forward or backward.
It names what we value, not how to deliver it.

**Who and why:** a household with Reolink cameras and Home Assistant that
wants to know what happens around the house without Reolink's own software.
That means two things, each with its own intent:

- [Looking](intent-card.md): "what happened around the house, and what's
  happening now?"
- [Being told](intent-alerts.md): "do I need to look at this?"

## Whose needs

Our own household is the guide. What we need is our best evidence of what
others need, and the product must meet our needs exactly.

## Better means

- **Less tied to us.** Less of the product depends on the particulars of our
  home: which cameras we have, what they're called, where they point, who
  lives here. Those belong in a household's settings.
- **More households fit.** More of the setups that can be built from the same
  kind of hardware work: more or fewer cameras, different models, different
  places and people, a different mix of what each household cares about.
- **Less to set up.** Another household with the same kind of hardware gets
  further with less effort and less knowledge of how it works inside.
- **One whole.** Looking and being told agree with each other: the same
  event, the same names, the same times.

## Floors

- Nothing here ever makes our own household's experience worse to serve
  someone else's.
- Nothing about a particular household is needed in this repo for the product
  to work.

## Not progress

- Hardware we don't own. We have no way to test it, so matching what we have
  (Reolink cameras and recorder, Home Assistant) is enough.
