# 2026-11-01: Prior art for camera review and live viewing on a phone

Survey of 12 camera/NVR apps, scored against the use cases in
`camera-card/docs/intent.md` (UC1 noise just now, UC2 overnight, UC3 doorbell
this morning, UC4 place right now, UC5 one event weeks ago, UC6 when a person
left).

Method: vendor help pages, release notes, App Store listing screenshots
(pulled through the iTunes lookup API), and recent US App Store reviews (RSS
feed, mostly Sep–Oct 2026). Reviews are anecdotes, not measurements. Anything
marked **unverified** is my inference or comes from one third-party source.
Every image's exact URL is in the [image sources](#image-sources) table at the end.

---

## Reolink (our cameras' own app)

- **Picking a camera:** a home list of big live tiles, one per camera. Each tile has bell, playback and settings icons ([listing](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id995927563)).
- **Moving through time:** pick a date, drag the timeline to get close, then scroll the video strip for the exact clip ([Reolink: search recordings](https://support.reolink.com/articles/900000657026-How-to-Search-Recordings/)). The Sept 2026 release (about v4.63) replaced chunked clips with a continuous, pinch-to-zoom 24h bar. Event blocks are coloured on the bar, and a filmstrip of clips sits above it (screenshot below).
- **Dense vs long:** the new 24h bar shows event density as coloured blocks. One reviewer says that at the default zoom you can't tell what caused a recording "until you zoom too far" (App Store review, 2026-09-14).
- **Filtering:** an AI filter for any motion, person, vehicle or pet. The newer build makes you tick boxes and then press OK, where the old one filtered on a single tap, and a reviewer called that "so much less usable" (review, 2026-10-01). Separately, "AI Video Search" lets you search events by object type ([Reolink](https://support.reolink.com/articles/44949969778713-Introduction-to-Reolink-AI-Video-Search/)).
- **Saving a clip:** a download icon at the top right saves to Photos. "Select" handles several clips at once. For big files, Reolink suggests using the desktop client ([Reolink](https://support.reolink.com/hc/en-us/articles/360015912514-How-to-Playback-and-Download-Cloud-Recordings-via-Reolink-App/)).
- **Notable:** reviews of the redesign are split. Some call it "spectacular… quick to get to the exact time" (2026-09-22). Others say it "butchered" quick clip-to-clip tapping (2026-09-23). Another regression: you can no longer swipe sideways to the next camera (2026-10-28). A late-Oct 2026 release broke live and playback on iOS 27.2 for many users.

## UniFi Protect

- **Picking a camera:** the home screen starts with a row of recent detection thumbnails ("People 12, Vehicles 23"), then camera tiles that show "Motion 3 minutes ago" ([listing](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id1392492235)).
- **Moving through time:** opening a camera starts live. A **vertical** timeline sits under the video, with LIVE at the top and older times further down. You drag down to rewind, and playback runs in real time when you let go (user description, [UI community](https://uisp.community.ui.com/questions/ea1c7a0d-7525-4ae8-827c-5e24413e32fc)). Event thumbnails sit beside the timeline. An "events only" mode collapses the empty time ([release notes](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id1392492235)).
- **Dense vs long:** continuous recording appears as the timeline, with each detection shown as a thumbnail. Protect 6.0 added per-camera object summaries and object-count charts on the timeline, plus "Spotlights" that surface key activity when you open the app ([blog](https://blog.ui.com/article/introducing-protect-6-0)).
- **Filtering:** "Find Anything" puts all the filters (person, vehicle, animal, audio, zones) in one view. Protect 6.0 lets you pause on a person and find them on every camera ([blog](https://blog.ui.com/article/introducing-protect-6-0)), which maps directly to UC6. Users report the smart-object filter sometimes returns zero results ([trunetto](https://www.trunetto.com/troubleshooting/security-cameras/unifi-protect/why-unifi-protect-timeline-missing-events)).
- **Saving a clip:** in the player, drag yellow start and end handles on the timeline, then tap download. There's also "Download Selected Clip" and long-press export on event thumbnails (release notes).
- **Notable:** fans say there are "no loading times… timeline type scrub" (2026-03-30). Critics say it takes ">30 s for video to appear" remotely (2026-10-01), that "Auto" quality always picks very low (2026-07-18), and that the timeline doesn't always show the current time (same review). Tapping a doorbell notification opens the clip rather than the live view (2026-08-04).

## Frigate (web UI / PWA)

- **Picking a camera:** a live dashboard grid. Each camera refreshes about once a minute and switches to a live stream when it sees motion ([0.14 release notes](https://github.com/blakeblackshear/frigate/discussions/12829)).
- **Moving through time:** **Review** shows a grid of short, low-res preview clips with a vertical timeline on the right. **History** shows a full-res scrubbable timeline, with previews of the other cameras alongside ([docs](https://docs.frigate.video/configuration/review/)).
- **Dense vs long:** activity is grouped into "review items", one per span of continuous activity, not one per object. Items are bucketed into **Alerts** (by default, a person or car) and **Detections** (everything else). The **Motion** tab scrubs all cameras at once with a motion-intensity bar, and can skip spans already covered by alerts ([docs](https://docs.frigate.video/configuration/review/)).
- **Filtering:** by camera, date, label and zone. "Show Reviewed" is a toggle. Hovering over or swiping a preview plays it **and marks it reviewed**.
- **Saving a clip:** export a range by dragging handles on the History timeline, or select review items and export them. Past exports live in a separate Exports pane.
- **Notable:** the timeline shading and the playback start time can disagree when motion comes before a detection ([#12177](https://github.com/blakeblackshear/frigate/discussions/12177)). A commenter on the release notes found alert review harder on a phone (unverified beyond that one comment).

[Frigate review](https://github.com/blakeblackshear/frigate/assets/569905/6fed96e8-3b18-40e5-9ddc-31e6f3c9f2ff) [Frigate motion scrubbing](https://github.com/blakeblackshear/frigate/assets/569905/d6788a15-0eeb-4427-a8d4-80b93cae3d74)
*Review grid with Alerts/Detections/Motion tabs and a time rail; multi-camera motion scrubbing. Source: [Frigate README](https://github.com/blakeblackshear/frigate).*

## Advanced Camera Card (HA): what we're replacing

- **Features:** live view, a media viewer, a gallery, and a timeline built on vis-timeline, with an event bar drawn per camera ([repo](https://github.com/dermotduffy/advanced-camera-card)). It also has a thumbnail carousel and filtering by zone and label.
- **Notable:** it's very flexible, which costs a lot of config surface. Our own fork (#299) is the evidence for the "less to it" goal. Its timeline is a desktop-style horizontal scrubber that is small on a phone (unverified, judged from the docs image).

## Ring

- **Picking a camera:** tap a camera preview on the dashboard. That opens Live View with the Event History timeline below it ([Ring help](https://ring.com/support/articles/16fks/Using-Event-History-Timeline)).
- **Moving through time:** slide the horizontal timeline left or right. A calendar jumps to a day, but historically not to a time of day (Ring staff, older [community thread](https://community.ring.com/t/event-history-list/2328)). With 24/7 recording, events show as markers on the continuous timeline ([Ring 24/7](https://ring.com/support/articles/wtr7r/24-7-recording-security-cams)).
- **Dense vs long:** History is a list of event cards. Ring has made those cards bigger more than once, and each time users complain about seeing fewer per screen: "about five events instead of a dozen" ([community](https://community.ring.com/t/event-history-display-has-changed-snapshots-not-appearing-now/177760)). Another review asks to "Bring back the small thumbnail" (2026-10-26).
- **Filtering:** History has filters for device, event type (motion, person, package, animal, vehicle, ring) and more.
- **Saving a clip:** share or download from the event (not covered on the help page above; unverified detail).
- **Notable:** since about Oct 2025, opening an event locks you to *that camera*. Users lost the ability to "watch ALL cameras sequentially", which mattered when "an event on one camera… another would pick up next" (review, 2026-10-07). An Oct 2026 update froze playback (audio only) until it was fixed.

*No useful official screenshot: the App Store images show only alarm and device screens.*

## Google Home (Nest)

- **Picking a camera:** camera tiles on the Home or Favorites tab. The **Activity** tab is a home-wide feed with Devices / Events / Date filter chips, topped by a Gemini "Home Brief" text summary of the day ([listing](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id680819774)).
- **Moving through time:** in a camera, the player sits on top and a **vertical** timeline with event thumbnails runs below it. Swipe left or right to switch between the timeline and an event list. Double-tap to skip ±10 s "like YouTube". Pull down on the control bar to resize the player ([Google blog](https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/devices/google-nest/google-home-app-gemini-redesign/), [Nest community](https://www.googlenestcommunity.com/t5/Blog/Camera-Performance-Improvements-in-the-Google-Home-app/ba-p/737343)).
- **Dense vs long:** with 24/7 history, white markers show events, grey means no activity, and gaps mean the camera was offline ([Google help](https://support.google.com/googlenest/answer/10904254)). A paid tier adds AI event descriptions in the timeline.
- **Filtering:** event filters (person, familiar face, vehicle, animal, package, sound) **persist across app restarts**, and the UI warns that some events are hidden while a filter is on ([Google help](https://support.google.com/googlenest/answer/10904254)). "Ask Home" handles natural-language search.
- **Saving a clip:** select and save or share from history (unverified on iOS specifics).
- **Notable:** reviews are harsh about slow and janky history: "painfully slow" (2026-10-29), "cannot pause or scrub slowly" (2026-10-24), and "Google Home only allows you to go back to events", unlike the old Nest app's continuous scroll-back (2026-10-27).

## Apple Home (HomeKit Secure Video)

- **Picking a camera:** a Cameras grid on the Home tab. Each tile shows a live snapshot with its age ("31s").
- **Moving through time:** swipe the timeline strip at the bottom of the camera view. Tap a clip to play it, and tap **Live** to return ([Apple, Mac guide](https://support.apple.com/en-my/guide/home/hmeb54708fc8/mac)). A bar shows which day you're on. Only 10 days are kept ([AppleInsider](https://appleinsider.com/articles/19/11/15/how-to-use-apples-homekit-secure-video-in-ios-13)).
- **Dense vs long:** it records clips only, with no 24/7. Each clip shows an **icon for what triggered it** (person, animal, vehicle, package). With Face Recognition on, the person's **name appears as you scrub** past them ([Apple](https://support.apple.com/en-my/guide/home/hmeb54708fc8/mac)). That's the closest thing in the survey to UC6.
- **Filtering:** you choose which kinds of motion get *recorded*. There's no filter for clips already recorded (no source found).
- **Saving a clip:** the standard iOS share sheet: Save Video to Photos, Save to Files, AirDrop ([9to5Mac](https://9to5mac.com/2019/11/15/homekit-secure-video-what-to-know/), [Macworld](https://www.macworld.com/article/1388132/how-to-export-video-homekit-secure-video-camera.html)).
- **Notable:** "harder to scrub" in iOS 27 (review, 2026-10-17). A loading spinner can stay over a clip that is already playing (2026-03-28).

## Eufy (Anker eufy)

- **Picking a camera:** device cards on Home. The Events tab is a cross-camera list with a date stepper (‹ July 20 ›) and a filter icon ([listing](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id1424956516)).
- **Moving through time:** event list, plus per-camera playback timeline. A "Security Report" shows a **per-hour activity histogram** across all devices for the chosen day.
- **Filtering:** by date, device, event type and storage location ([eufy](https://service.eufy.com/article-description/Introduction-to-New-eufy-App)). Human, vehicle and pet filters depend on the camera model (third-party claim, unverified).
- **Saving a clip:** open the event, then download to the album ([eufy](https://service.eufy.com/article-description/Download-and-Delete-Videos-via-eufy-App)). Delete also wipes the copy on the device.
- **Notable:** "won't play recordings from Events, only from notifications" (2026-10-30). Timeline markers vanished after an update ([community](https://community.eufy.com/t/cant-access-playback-entries-on-eufy-security-app/3603517)).

## Arlo

- **Picking a camera:** dashboard tiles. The **Feed** (formerly Library) is a chronological, cross-camera list with a label (Person, Vehicle) and a thumbnail on each row.
- **Moving through time:** a calendar icon jumps to a day. The old Library put a **month calendar with dots on days that have clips** ([Arlo KB](https://kb.arlo.com/1030523/How-do-I-use-the-library-in-my-Arlo-account)).
- **Filtering:** Feed Settings › Event Types, Devices, recognised people and vehicles ([Arlo](https://www.arlo.com/en_gb/support/faq/000062807/how-do-I-filter-events-and-video-recordings-in-my-arlo-feed)). Filters are buried in settings, not one tap away.
- **Saving a clip:** "Share" sends a *link*. You have to download first to get the actual file ([AndroidHeadlines](https://www.androidheadlines.com/how-to-save-share-footage-arlo-cameras.html)).
- **Notable:** "several hundred clips a day… good luck finding what you need"; "you can't filter at the time you are looking" (2026-10-26). "Feed needs device/date filter" (2026-09-21).

## Synology DS cam

- **Picking a camera:** a camera list or a multi-view grid (up to 2×3 synchronized playback) ([Synology spec](https://www.synology.com/en-us/dsm/7.4/software_spec/surveillance_station)).
- **Moving through time:** a horizontal timeline with **event-only mode**, "last/next event" buttons, and ±10 s seek. A playlist of events sits below. The **Smart Timeline** changes zoom with the length of your swipe: a short flick moves seconds, a long swipe moves hours ([DS cam help](https://kb.synology.com/en-us/SurveillanceStation/help/DScam/Android)).
- **Filtering:** "advanced filters" in the event list. Instant Search (person, vehicle, motion within an area you draw) works only with Synology's own cameras. Desktop-only Smart Search isn't available in the app.
- **Saving a clip:** download to Photos. Reviewers note it needs full photo-library access, and that H.265 couldn't be saved on iOS (2023 reviews).
- **Notable:** "doesn't show previews [while scrubbing], only starts playing when stopping" (2025-10-10). "Easier to download all footage… than scrub in this app" (2024-11-03).

## Blue Iris (iOS app + UI3 web)

- **Picking a camera:** camera list. UI3 deep links (`?tab=clips&cam=porch`) let you bookmark one camera's view ([UI3 help](https://bp2008.github.io/ui3/ui3/help/help.html)).
- **Moving through time:** the iOS app shows a **month calendar above a clip list**. UI3 has a timeline tab. The UI3 author once argued that the clip list "is already a sort of timeline" ([wiki](https://github.com/bp2008/ui3/wiki/Potential-Features)).
- **Filtering:** views for all, alerts, confirmed (AI) alerts, flagged and new. The clip search box accepts AND/OR/NOT logic (BI 6.1.4.13, [changelog](https://blueirissoftware.com/changelog6.pdf)). Third-party "Sightline" adds People/Vehicles/Animals buttons and a timeline with coloured alert markers ([repo](https://github.com/rw377/Sightline-for-Blue-Iris)), which suggests the stock app lacks them.
- **Saving a clip:** export from the clip list. Timeline export in UI3 only landed in Oct 2026. Saving to the camera roll broke for some users on BI 5 (review, 2025-05-18).
- **Notable:** "can't scrub to a specific spot… without it jumping back to the start" (2023). The interface is dated and "doesn't follow iOS guidelines" (2024).

## Scrypted NVR

- **Picking a camera:** the home screen is a **horizontal strip of recent event thumbnails over full-width live camera tiles** ([listing](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id1658171214)).
- **Moving through time:** the player is on top and a **vertical timeline** runs below it, with detection thumbnails placed at their time and small icons for person, car and animal. A floating "Play" button and a date chip sit over it.
- **Filtering:** an Events search view shows a **grid of cropped detection thumbnails** with face-recognition tags ("Kid Mom") and one-tap icons for person, animal, vehicle and package. Tapping a "Story" (AI summary) jumps to that moment on the timeline ([docs](https://docs.scrypted.app/scrypted-nvr/ai-summaries.html)).
- **Saving a clip:** a share icon in the player (seen in the screenshot; details unverified).
- **Notable:** the closest design to "phone-first" in the survey. A bad release in Oct 2026 broke detections for a few hours ([issue #41](https://github.com/koush/nvr.scrypted.app/issues/41)).

## Wyze

- **Picking a camera:** device list, or an Events tab that covers every camera.
- **Moving through time:** the Events tab has a **week strip** (S M T W T F S) and Person/Faces chips at the top. The **multi-cam timeline** puts one horizontal lane per camera, with coloured event blocks and a shared live cursor ([listing](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id1288415553)).
- **Filtering:** funnel › motion, sound, camera. Person, vehicle, pet and package filters require Cam Plus ([Wyze](https://support.wyze.com/hc/en-us/articles/6658691642011-How-do-I-use-the-Wyze-app)).
- **Notable:** to see an event's neighbours you have to "back out, Events tab, find the Event… manually" ([forum](https://forums.wyze.com/t/multi-cam-timeline-how-do-you-use-it/324298)). There's still no continuous swipe between cameras, seven years after users first asked (2026-10-08). Scrubbing freezes "every single time" (2026-10-11).

## Patterns worth stealing

| # | Pattern | Seen in | Helps |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Live at the top of a vertical timeline.** Live is just "now" on the same scroll, so getting back to the present is one flick. | UniFi, Google Home, Scrypted | UC4, UC1; "less distance between past and present" |
| 2 | **Player pinned above a vertical, thumb-scrolled timeline**, with event thumbnails placed at their time. Feels like a phone feed, not a desktop scrubber. | UniFi, Google Home, Scrypted | UC1, UC3; "at home on a phone" |
| 3 | **Cross-camera "what just happened" strip** of recent detection thumbnails above the live tiles. Answers "which camera?" before you choose one. | UniFi, Scrypted, Frigate | UC1 |
| 4 | **Group a burst into one item** (one review item per span of activity) and bucket items by importance (people and cars vs the rest). | Frigate | UC2, UC3; "calmer", "less time to understand" |

## Pitfalls

- **Event-only history with no continuous scroll-back.** Google Home users miss the Nest app's continuous scroll-back. If detection missed it, you can't find it (UC1, UC5).
- **Filters that take two steps** (tick boxes, then OK) or live in settings. Reolink got a backlash for the first and Arlo for the second (Arlo's are buried in Feed Settings).
- **Filters that silently hide events or return nothing.** UniFi, Wyze and Synology all have "filter shows zero" complaints. Always show that a filter is active and how much it hides.
- **Locking playback to one camera.** Ring's 2025 change forces you back out to follow an intruder from camera to camera.
- **Bigger cards, fewer per screen.** Ring's redesigns each cut events per screen from about 12 to about 5, and users noticed every time.
- **Scrubbing that shows nothing until you let go**, or snaps back to the start: DS cam, Blue Iris. Scrubbing should show frames or thumbnails as you go.
- **Low "Auto" quality with no sticky override** (UniFi), and replays served at full resolution over cellular (DS cam). Pick quality deliberately.
- **Overlays that cover the video:** spinners that stay over a playing clip (Apple Home), controls that never hide (Wyze), landscape layouts where the controls eat the frame (Blue Iris).
- **Notification → clip instead of live** for a doorbell (UniFi). It doesn't apply to us (answering the door is a non-goal), but the general point does: a tap from "something is happening" should land on *now*.
- **Big-bang redesigns.** Reolink, Ring, Google and Eufy reviews all spike after layout overhauls, even the good ones. Calm, incremental change beats a rewrite.
- **"Share" that sends a cloud link** instead of the file (Arlo). That's no help to us; we want the actual video file.
- **Text summaries in place of video** (Google Home Brief, Scrypted Stories): interesting for UC2, but they depend on an LLM and are out of scope for "less to it" (my judgement).
